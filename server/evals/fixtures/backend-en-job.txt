Senior Backend Engineer

About us
Northbeam Logistics builds real-time tracking and routing software for
freight carriers. We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to help scale our
core API platform.

Responsibilities
- Design and maintain REST and event-driven APIs serving high-throughput
  logistics data.
- Operate and improve our Kubernetes-based deployment platform on AWS (EKS).
- Own PostgreSQL schema design and query performance for multi-tenant data.
- Collaborate with the platform team on CI/CD and observability.
- Mentor junior engineers and participate in architecture reviews.

Requirements
- 4+ years of backend engineering experience.
- Strong experience with Node.js or Python in production.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes in production (required).
- Solid understanding of PostgreSQL and relational data modeling.
- Experience with message queues (RabbitMQ, Kafka, or similar).

Nice to have
- AWS certifications.
- Experience with Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tools.
- Experience mentoring other engineers.

Employment type: Full-time
Work mode: Remote (EU timezones)
